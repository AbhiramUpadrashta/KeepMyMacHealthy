Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No trial, no subscription, no ads. It's made by Bubbles Aro and released under the MIT License.

Is it safe to clean caches?

Caches are temporary files apps recreate when needed. KeepMyMacHealthy skips caches macOS depends on, and everything goes to the Trash first.

Why does it ask for Full Disk Access?

macOS protects Mail, Safari and some app data. With Full Disk Access the scan can see and clean those too. Without it, everything else still works.

Why does it ask for my password?

Only for system maintenance like freeing RAM, reindexing Spotlight or removing system-wide adware. The prompt is macOS's own; the app never sees your password.

How do updates work?

Settings → Check for Updates downloads the newest release, verifies its signature and installs it in place.
